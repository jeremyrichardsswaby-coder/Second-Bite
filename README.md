# Second Bite 🍽️

Second Bite tackles global food waste by offering affordable surplus meals without sacrificing quality or customer support. A better, more reliable way to rescue great food.

---

## 🌍 The Problem
* **Massive Global Food Waste:** Millions of tons of edible food go to waste every year across restaurants, bakeries, and grocery stores.
* **Flawed Existing Solutions:** While surplus food apps exist, customers frequently deal with poor food quality, mystery items that don't meet expectations, and frustrating customer service when things go wrong.

## 💡 Our Solution
**Second Bite** bridges the gap between sustainability and customer satisfaction. We partner with local food businesses to offer surplus meals at a fraction of the retail price—with standards users can actually trust.

### Key Value Propositions
* **Guaranteed Food Quality:** No more gambling on stale or inedible mystery bags. Every meal meets strict freshness and quality standards.
* **Low, Accessible Pricing:** Enjoy great food at heavily discounted prices while helping local businesses recover costs on unsold inventory.
* **Reliable Customer Service & Fair Refunds:** Dedicated support and a transparent, hassle-free refund policy whenever an order falls short.

## 🚀 Why It Works
* **For Consumers:** Saves money on high-quality meals with zero risk thanks to quality guarantees and buyer protection.
* **For Businesses:** Turns potential food waste into extra revenue while building lasting trust with repeat customers.
* **For the Planet:** Directly reduces landfill waste and the environmental footprint of food production.
